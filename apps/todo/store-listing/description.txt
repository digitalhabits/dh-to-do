Get back to that thing you meant to do. Digital Habits: To-Do keeps you on track with simple, beautiful task lists and powerful features built for distraction-free productivity:

- Focus Mode: pop out your task in a floating window, complete with a timer
- Minimalistic to-do lists: use tabs to manage multiple lists (e.g. Work, Personal) of tasks organised by context
- Stay on track: assign time to tasks and see time spent on tasks in a collapsible history
- Built-in integrations with your tools: sync tasks two-way with Apple Reminders and Basecamp (Google Tasks integration coming soon!)
- Calming, Danish-style calendar view: month/week view of tasks and goals, with calendar integration
- Board view: organise your tasks in columns for Today, Soon (-ish) and Backlog. Make 'Today' a satisfying and simple full-screen view with one click
- Subtasks for big tasks: break big and complicated tasks up into subtasks that make it easy to stay on track
- Data backup and restore: export and import your data for backup or moving to another machine

The source code of Digital Habits: To-Do is public, and the app collects zero user data.
Source code: https://github.com/digitalhabits/dh-to-do
