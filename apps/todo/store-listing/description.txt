Get back to that thing you meant to do. Digital Habits: To-Do keeps you on track with simple, beautiful task lists and features built for distraction-free work:

- Focus Mode: pop your task out into a floating window with a timer, with up to two open at a time
- Simple lists: use tabs for your lists (e.g. Work, Personal), and gather tabs into groups. Turn groups on in Settings
- Tasks with the details you need: subtasks, due dates, and notes with pictures
- Stay on track: give tasks a time and see the time you spent on each
- Board View: sort your tasks into Backlog, Soon (-ish) and Today, and press Start on Today for a calm, full-screen view that takes you through them one by one. Turn it on in Settings
- Work with others: assign tasks to people and filter the board by person. Turn it on in Settings
- Calendar View: a calm, Danish-style month or week calendar of your tasks and goals, with your own calendars linked in. Turn it on in Settings
- Sync two ways with Basecamp, and with Apple Reminders on a Mac (Google Tasks integration coming soon!)
- Light and dark themes, in English or Danish
- Back up and restore: export your data, pictures included, and import it on another computer

The source code of Digital Habits: To-Do is public, and the app collects no personal data. It sends one anonymous count a day, with an ID that changes every month, so we know how many people use it. You can turn this off in Settings.
Source code: https://github.com/digitalhabits/dh-to-do
